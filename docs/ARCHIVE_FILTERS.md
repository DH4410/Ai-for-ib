## Older archives and marking continuity

The tutor accepts indexed paper years from 2000 onward, includes legacy
science Paper 1 in its practice selector, and supports numeric timezones
and TZA/TZB/TZC. Older papers may follow an earlier syllabus; filtering
does not certify that their content is in the current syllabus.

Marking uses only the selected exact question when pinned and refuses
visual-dependent questions in the text-only workflow. A paired question
with an official maximum can receive a suggested mark even when topic
classification is missing, but cannot update mastery until a verified
topic mapping exists. Topic selectors for real-paper attempts offer only
the mapped topics.

## Account isolation in the browser

A single authentication subscription owns the workspace identity. Switching accounts or signing out remounts the workspace, clearing in-memory turns, drafts, selected question IDs and progress/source views. Token refreshes for the same account preserve the workspace. Requests are bound to the session from that workspace; superseded or unmounted requests are aborted and stale completions ignored. PDF blob URLs are revoked on close/unmount. This does not undo an already completed database write or retract PDFs that the learner separately downloaded.
